Nyx Ulric

Kingsglaive | Protector of Lucis | Warrior of Galahd

About

Nyx Ulric is a legendary member of the Kingsglaive, known for his fierce loyalty, relentless courage, and willingness to stand between danger and the people he protects. Born in Galahd and forged by war, he carries both deep scars and unshakable resolve.

Whether on the battlefield or in the heart of Insomnia, Nyx stands as a symbol of sacrifice, strength, and devotion. This site is a tribute to the man, the myth, and the blade that never broke.
